Behind the music

Meet DkRapria

DkRapria is an independent Indian singer, songwriter, composer and music producer from Haryana, creating original Hindi and Haryanvi songs alongside devotional music. His work spans emotional love songs, regional music and faith-inspired releases, bringing storytelling and melody together.

Working across songwriting, composition, music production and video creation, DkRapria develops original music and presents each release through official artist channels. Explore the song catalogue and listen on Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Gaana and JioSaavn.
