About DkRapria

Learn about independent Indian music artist DkRapria and the music featured on this official website.

Independent music from Haryana

DkRapria is an independent Indian singer, songwriter, composer and music producer from Haryana. The catalogue brings together original Hindi songs, Haryanvi music, emotional love songs and devotional releases.

Hindi songs and emotional storytelling

Hindi releases explore themes such as relationships, memory, heartbreak and personal emotion. The songs are presented through individual release pages so listeners can identify a track and follow its official streaming links.

Haryanvi music and regional identity

Haryanvi releases draw on the language and cultural identity of Haryana. The catalogue includes regional songs alongside contemporary Hindi releases, giving listeners more than one musical direction to explore.

Devotional music

The devotional catalogue includes bhajans and faith-inspired music connected with Hindu devotional traditions. These releases are presented separately so listeners can find devotional tracks and open the corresponding official music platform.

Where to listen

Official releases are linked from this website to YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Gaana and JioSaavn. Visit the song catalogue or the streaming links on the homepage.
