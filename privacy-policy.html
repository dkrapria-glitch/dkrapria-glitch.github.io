Privacy Policy

How the DkRapria Official website handles information and third-party services.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

About this website

This is the official website of independent music artist DkRapria. It provides artist information, original music catalogue details, cover artwork and links to official music and social platforms.

Information collected by this website

This static website does not provide visitor accounts, user comments, newsletter subscriptions or a website-hosted payment form. It does not intentionally ask visitors to submit personal information through this site. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of hosting, security and service operation. Please review GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

Third-party links and embedded services

Pages may link to services such as YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, Amazon Music, Gaana, JioSaavn and Instagram. When you follow one of these links, that service’s own privacy policy and cookie practices apply. This website does not control how third-party services collect or use information.

Advertising and cookies

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled on this website in the future, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to deliver, measure and personalise advertising, subject to applicable settings and law. This policy will be updated before advertising is activated to describe the services actually in use. Visitors can learn how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services at Google’s partner-sites information page.

Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and manage advertising personalisation through the relevant third-party provider. Blocking some cookies may affect features offered by third-party websites.

Contact

For privacy questions about this website, contact DkRapria through the official contact page.
