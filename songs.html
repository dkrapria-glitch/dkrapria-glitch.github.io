Explore the DkRapria music catalogue

DkRapria is an independent music artist from Haryana. This catalogue brings together releases across contemporary Hindi music, Haryanvi songs and devotional music. It is organised by release type so listeners can browse singles, devotional releases, EPs and albums without confusing one category with another.

Hindi songs and emotional music

The Hindi catalogue includes romantic, reflective and emotional song titles. These releases are presented as individual entries so listeners can identify the recording they want before opening a music service. Song titles and artwork are retained as release identifiers.

Haryanvi music

The Haryanvi releases represent the artist’s regional-language work and Haryana-inspired themes. The catalogue keeps these tracks alongside the wider discography while the category filters make them easier to find by title and release type.

Bhakti and devotional releases

The devotional section contains bhajans and faith-oriented music. These releases are grouped separately from romantic and regional songs to make devotional listening easier and to keep the catalogue clearly structured.

About listening links: Buttons marked “Search” open a platform search for the release title and artist. They are not represented as confirmed direct track URLs. Direct play links are used only when the exact release destination is available. Streaming availability may vary by country and platform.
